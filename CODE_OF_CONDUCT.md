# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ARVREL a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and safe engineering practice.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publication of private information, deliberate disclosure of confidential operational data, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route on the `masarray` GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent restriction according to the impact and pattern of behaviour.
