Trust before trip

Visibility does not automatically grant operation permission.

ARVREL separates diagnostic visibility, measurement admission, pickup evaluation, and virtual-trip authority so unhealthy communication or configuration evidence remains explicit.

Permission model

Three gates describe what the current evidence allows.

AllowsMeasurement

Decoded quantities may enter the measurement pipeline and contribute to complete RMS, waveform, and phasor windows.

AllowsPickup

Protection pickup and timer behavior may be evaluated and presented for engineering diagnosis.

AllowsTrip

An operated element may assert the virtual trip latch and create a new operation record.

Important: a stream may remain visible for diagnosis while recent continuity, quality, SCL, or scaling evidence prevents a new virtual trip.

Evidence inputs

Trust is built from transport, configuration, and measurement context.

Transport health

Live freshness, payload decode, complete ASDU handling, smpCnt continuity, duplicates, out-of-order frames, and stream recovery.

Configuration identity

Destination address, APPID, VLAN, svID, dataset, confRev, SCL binding, channel mapping, and engineering scaling provenance.

Measurement readiness

Complete one-cycle protection windows, complete two-cycle evidence windows, available current/voltage channels, and directional polarizing quantities.

Operation evidence

Preserve the decision context, not only the trip label.

What the engine records

  • Pickup and trip timestamps
  • Operated element and phase/earth cause
  • Operating quantity and unit
  • Directional angle and polarizing magnitude where applicable
  • Active settings group, revision, and fingerprint
  • SMV trust state at operation

What the reviewer should add

  • ARVREL version and package type
  • Input-source description and ownership
  • SCL and CT/VT context
  • Scenario purpose and expected result
  • Known platform and validation limitations
  • Exported event or JSON evidence

Evidence boundary

Software evidence is not certification evidence.

ARVREL evidence supports review and reproducibility inside a controlled laboratory context. It is not calibrated relay-test evidence, IEC 60255 type-test evidence, IEC 61850 conformance certification, commissioning approval, or switching authority.
