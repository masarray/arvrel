Engineering boundary

Virtual-output laboratory software with explicit limits.

ARVREL is designed for education, source review, controlled laboratory evaluation, FAT/SAT preparation, troubleshooting, and research. It is not an operational protection IED or calibrated relay test set.

ARVREL must not be treated as switching authority, commissioning acceptance, or protection-grade real-time equipment. No screen, event, export, or virtual trip produced by ARVREL authorizes operation of primary plant.
Intended

Controlled engineering use

  • Education and university laboratories
  • Source review and software regression
  • Authorized process-bus troubleshooting
  • FAT/SAT preparation and demonstration rehearsal
  • Protection-algorithm research with synthetic or owned data
Not provided

Operational or certified authority

  • Physical relay contacts
  • Operational GOOSE trip or MMS control
  • Autonomous switching or interlocking approval
  • IEC 61850 conformance certification
  • IEC 60255 type-test or calibration evidence
  • Hard-real-time protection guarantee

Known public-beta limitations

Current constraints must travel with every result.

Platform and live performance

  • Windows scheduling, host load, Npcap, adapter drivers, and publisher behavior influence live performance.
  • The public beta is not a hard-real-time platform.
  • Unsigned community binaries may trigger Windows reputation warnings.
  • Clean-machine, multi-adapter, and long-duration validation remains ongoing.

Protection scope

  • 46, 47, 49, 81U/O, 32, 37, 50BF, 79, 25, 86, 74TCS, and 60 are not implemented.
  • Negative-sequence and memory polarization for 67N are deferred.
  • Feeder elements default disabled until explicitly configured.
  • Custom algorithm source remains validation-and-shadow staging only.

Data handling

Keep customer and station material private.

Do not publish

Customer captures, proprietary SCL, station names, IP plans, credentials, employer-confidential settings, or evidence that identifies protected infrastructure.

Use instead

Synthetic, sanitized, contributor-owned, or explicitly authorized fixtures with non-sensitive names and reproducible scenario documentation.

Local data: preferences and crash logs may include local paths or selected file context under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ARVREL. Review and redact diagnostics before sharing.

Security and reporting

Use the correct disclosure route.

Report reproducible non-sensitive defects through GitHub Issues. Report vulnerabilities through the private security-advisory process. Never attach confidential station material to a public issue.
