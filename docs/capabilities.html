Product capability map

One workspace for the protection cause-and-effect chain.

ARVREL connects process-bus capture, identity, measurement, protection, trust permission, and operation evidence while keeping each engineering boundary visible.

Process bus

Inspect live and recorded Sampled Values.

Use Npcap live capture or replay PCAP and PCAPNG files. ARVREL exposes stream identity and transport evidence before those samples influence measurement or protection.

Acquisition

Live Npcap capture

Select a Windows adapter and inspect authorized IEC 61850 Sampled Values traffic on an isolated laboratory network.

Reproducibility

PCAP / PCAPNG replay

Replay recorded frames for deterministic investigation, regression work, teaching, and controlled demonstrations.

Identity

SCL-assisted binding

Evaluate APPID, destination MAC, VLAN, svID, dataset, confRev, channel mapping, and scaling provenance.

Measurement

Convert complete windows into coherent engineering quantities.

Protection quantities use the newest complete one-cycle window. Waveform evidence uses complete two-cycle windows and can hold the last coherent frame during stream recovery.

4I + 4V channels

IA, IB, IC, IN/3I0 and VA, VB, VC, VN/3V0 with secondary and primary CT/VT display context.

Phasors and sequences

Fundamental RMS phasors plus positive-, negative-, and zero-sequence quantities for protection and analysis.

Residual provenance

Explicit decoded neutral/residual channels are preferred, with clearly identified phase-sum fallback where required.
