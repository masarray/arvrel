Product model

Observe. Evaluate. Prove.

ARVREL makes the complete protection cause-and-effect chain visible without hiding the engineering assumptions between packet evidence and virtual operation.

01

Observe

Inspect stream identity, continuity, quality, mapping, scaling, waveform, phasors, residual channels, and symmetrical components.

02

Evaluate

Apply native settings and review pickup, timing, directional decisions, blocking, operation, and the virtual trip latch.

03

Prove

Preserve active settings identity, trust state, timestamps, operating quantity, trip cause, event trace, and exportable evidence.
