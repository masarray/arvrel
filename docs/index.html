Trust architecture

Visibility does not automatically grant trip permission.

A stream may remain available for diagnosis while communication or configuration evidence removes permission for a new virtual operation. ARVREL evaluates complete windows, freshness, decode health, smpCnt continuity, quality, mapping, scaling provenance, SCL binding, address identity, svID, dataset, and confRev consistency.

Rejected duplicate and out-of-order frames remain visible in telemetry, but their payload samples do not enter measurement or protection buffers.

Engineering boundary

Virtual-output laboratory software.

ARVREL is not a certified protection IED or calibrated relay test set. It provides no physical contact, operational GOOSE trip, MMS control, autonomous switching, switching authority, IEC 61850 conformance certification, IEC 60255 type-test evidence, or hard-real-time guarantee. Use live capture only on isolated, authorized laboratory networks.
