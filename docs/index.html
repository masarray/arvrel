Engineering outcome

Observe. Evaluate. Prove.

ARVREL keeps the complete cause-and-effect chain visible instead of reducing protection analysis to a final pickup or trip lamp.

01

Observe

Inspect stream identity, continuity, quality, mapping, scaling, waveform, phasors, residual channels, and sequence quantities.

02

Evaluate

Apply native settings and review pickup, timing, directional decisions, transformer restraint/security, blocking, operation, and the virtual trip latch.

03

Prove

Preserve settings identity, trust state, timestamps, operating quantity, trip cause, event trace, and exportable evidence.

Why ARVREL is different

Visibility, authority, and evidence stay separate.

Trust architecture

Visible does not automatically mean trippable

AllowsMeasurement, AllowsPickup, and AllowsTrip make degraded communication and configuration evidence explicit.

Reproducibility

Public claims map to deterministic tests

Stable scenario identifiers connect engineering statements to exact source files, test files, and named test methods. Transformer beta.3 also exposes a 10-scenario packaged-core self-test.

Bounded claims

Laboratory capability without operational overclaim

ARVREL states what is implemented, what remains deferred, and what is not certification, calibration, conformance, or hard-real-time evidence.

Clear engineering boundary

Virtual-output laboratory software.

ARVREL is not a certified protection IED or calibrated relay test set. It provides no physical contact, operational GOOSE trip, MMS control, autonomous switching, switching authority, IEC 61850 conformance certification, IEC 60255 type-test evidence, or hard-real-time guarantee.
