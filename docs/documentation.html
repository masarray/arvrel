Documentation boundary

Documentation explains the current evidence; it does not expand product authority.

ARVREL remains virtual-output laboratory software. Documentation, tests, and public research do not constitute IEC 61850 conformance certification, IEC 60255 type-test evidence, calibration, commissioning acceptance, or switching authority.
