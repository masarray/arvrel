Task-oriented evaluation

Start from the engineering question, not the feature list.

ARVREL supports different users without pretending they share the same evidence needs. Choose the workflow closest to the decision you need to review.

Workflow routes

Four practical starting points.

Each route identifies the input, the work performed inside ARVREL, the evidence produced, and the boundary that remains outside the software.

Protection engineer

Evaluate settings and virtual operation

Review pickup, timing, direction, blocking, trip cause, active settings identity, and operation evidence.

 Substation automation

Investigate Sampled Values and trust

Inspect stream identity, SCL binding, continuity, quality, mapping, scaling, freshness, and admission into measurement.

 Integration team

Prepare FAT/SAT demonstrations

Use deterministic internal scenarios and authorized synthetic or recorded SV sources to prepare reviewable demonstrations.

 University and research

Teach and study protection behavior

Connect observable signals, measurements, timers, protection decisions, and immutable research artifacts.

Common discipline

Every workflow begins with provenance.

ARVREL does not treat visible data as automatically trustworthy for operation.

01Identify the source
02Confirm mapping and scaling
03Check continuity and quality
04Build complete measurement windows
05Evaluate protection logic
06Apply trust permission
07Preserve evidence
Operational boundary: these workflows support controlled laboratory evaluation and preparation. They do not create commissioning authority, calibrated test evidence, or permission to operate primary equipment.

Recommended entry

Validate the internal laboratory first.

Before connecting a live adapter or importing customer material, confirm the application behavior using the deterministic internal A-G scenario.
