Suggested laboratory modules

Build learning around observable cause and effect.

1. SV identity and SCL binding

Identify APPID, destination MAC, VLAN, svID, dataset, confRev, mapping, and scaling.

2. Fundamental RMS and phasor measurement

Compare waveform windows, the one-cycle single-bin DFT, residual-channel provenance, and symmetrical components.

3. Overcurrent timing

Study 50/51 pickup, dropout, definite delay, IEC inverse timing, TMS, and reset behavior.

4. Directional supervision

Explore V1/I1 and 3V0/3I0 polarization, direction, characteristic angle, torque, and minimum-voltage supervision.

5. Trust and restraint

Introduce gaps, quality flags, SCL mismatch, or unresolved scaling and observe measurement, pickup, and trip permissions.

6. Evidence and reproducibility

Export an operation record and demonstrate why version, scenario, source, settings, trust context, and limitations matter.

Algorithm laboratory

Research source remains separated from the executing standard algorithm.

The public beta exposes exact active standard source as read-only evidence. Editable custom source is policy-validated and staged as an immutable shadow artifact tied to settings and source hashes.

Standard active

Read-only source

Shadow artifact

Editable research source can be validated and staged, but it cannot replace runtime protection behavior.

Research boundary: the current validator is a staging policy, not a secure deterministic compiler or runtime sandbox. Custom runtime activation remains outside the public beta.

Publication practice

Use versioned, source-linked, and non-sensitive evidence.

  • Cite the exact ARVREL release or commit used.
  • Reference stable scenario identifiers from the machine-readable catalog.
  • Retain scenario and settings fingerprints.
  • Use synthetic or contributor-owned captures and SCL.
  • State that results are deterministic software laboratory evidence, not IEC 60255 type-test or calibrated performance evidence.
  • Use the repository CITATION.cff metadata where appropriate.
