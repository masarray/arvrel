University and research

Connect packet evidence, measurement, protection logic, and reproducible results.

ARVREL provides a transparent laboratory path from IEC 61850 Sampled Values to RMS, phasors, sequences, protection timers, trust permission, and virtual operation records.

Teach

Make the signal chain visible

Relate Ethernet identity and SCL context to channel values, phasors, symmetrical components, protection pickup, timers, and trip evidence.

Experiment

Change one variable at a time

Use deterministic internal scenarios or controlled replay to compare settings, source quality, continuity, direction, and voltage supervision.

Reproduce

Preserve software and settings identity

Record version, settings revision, SHA-256 fingerprint, input source, trust state, operation result, and exported evidence.

Suggested laboratory modules

Build learning around observable cause and effect.

1. SV identity and SCL binding

Identify APPID, destination MAC, VLAN, svID, dataset, confRev, mapping, and scaling.

2. RMS and phasor measurement

Compare waveform windows, one-cycle DFT quantities, residual channels, and symmetrical components.

3. Overcurrent timing

Study 50/51 pickup, dropout, definite delay, IEC inverse timing, TMS, and reset behavior.

4. Directional supervision

Explore V1/I1 and 3V0/3I0 polarization, direction, characteristic angle, and minimum voltage supervision.

5. Trust and restraint

Introduce gaps, quality flags, SCL mismatch, or unresolved scaling and observe measurement, pickup, and trip permissions.

6. Evidence and reproducibility

Export an operation record and demonstrate why version, source, settings, and trust context matter.

Algorithm laboratory

Research source remains separated from the executing standard algorithm.

The public beta exposes exact active standard source as read-only evidence. Editable custom source is policy-validated and staged as an immutable shadow artifact tied to settings and source hashes.

Standard active

The deterministic built-in protection implementation continues to execute.

Read-only source

The active standard algorithm source is visible for engineering review.

Shadow artifact

Editable research source can be validated and staged, but it cannot replace runtime protection behavior.

Research boundary: the current validator is a staging policy, not a secure deterministic compiler or runtime sandbox. Custom runtime activation remains outside the public beta.

Publication practice

Use versioned and non-sensitive evidence.

  • Cite the exact ARVREL release or commit used.
  • Retain scenario and settings fingerprints.
  • Use synthetic or contributor-owned captures and SCL.
  • State that results are software laboratory evidence, not IEC 60255 type-test or calibrated performance evidence.
  • Use the repository CITATION.cff metadata where appropriate.
