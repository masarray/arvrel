Application note AN-03

Direction is evaluated from explicit operating and polarizing phasors.

ARVREL uses positive-sequence V1/I1 quantities for 67P and residual 3V0/3I0 quantities for 67N. Direction is supervised by minimum polarizing voltage and selected from the sign of a cosine torque expression relative to the configured characteristic angle.

Inspect sourceOpen directional evidence

Quantity selection

67P and 67N use different measurement domains.

67P

Positive-sequence V1/I1

The operating phasor is positive-sequence current and the polarizing phasor is positive-sequence voltage. Pickup also requires the maximum phase current to reach the configured threshold.

67N

Residual 3V0/3I0

The operating phasor is residual current and the polarizing phasor is residual voltage. Explicit decoded neutral/residual channels are preferred over calculated phase-sum fallback.

Directional equation

The selected region is derived from operating angle and MTA.

operatingAngle = normalize(angle(Iop) - angle(Vpol))
torque         = cos(operatingAngle - characteristicAngle)
forwardRegion  = torque > 0
selected       = forwardRegion          when sense = Forward
                 not forwardRegion      when sense = Reverse

A directional decision becomes unavailable when the polarizing-voltage magnitude is below the configured minimum. Current pickup, selected direction, elapsed delay, SMV pickup permission, and SMV trip permission remain separate conditions.

Operate chain

A directional trip requires more than positive torque.

Evidence output

Direction is preserved as review context.

The directional decision records polarizing availability, selected direction, operating angle, torque, operating magnitude, polarizing magnitude, and a readable reason string. The protection operation record also preserves operated element, phase or earth attribution, timestamps, quantity, settings identity, and trust state.

Engineering value: reviewers can see why the element considered a case forward or reverse rather than receiving only a binary trip indication.

Current boundary

This is a transparent beta implementation, not a complete vendor relay characteristic.

Not currently implemented

  • Memory polarization
  • Negative-sequence polarization for 67N
  • Phase-specific cross polarization
  • Load encroachment or power-swing supervision
  • Directional comparison schemes
  • Type-tested relay characteristic tolerances

Future validation track

  • Angle sweep across forward/reverse boundaries
  • Minimum-voltage supervision sweep
  • Residual-channel provenance cases
  • Noise and unbalance sensitivity
  • Off-nominal frequency effects
  • Hardware-in-the-loop timing comparison
