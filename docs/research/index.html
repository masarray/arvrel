Verified method

Fundamental phasors use a one-cycle single-bin DFT—not a full FFT.

The estimator requires a complete one-cycle sample window, removes the window mean, correlates the samples with fundamental-frequency sine and cosine terms, and scales the complex result to RMS. This is sufficient for the implemented fundamental phasor and sequence calculations without claiming a harmonic-spectrum FFT.

Window

Complete one cycle

The newest complete one-cycle window is required before the estimator produces a protection phasor.

Estimator

DC-removed fundamental bin

The sample mean is removed before cosine/sine correlation at one nominal fundamental bin.

Result

Complex RMS phasor

The result supports magnitude, phase, residual quantities, and positive-, negative-, and zero-sequence calculations.

Related work

ARVREL is comparable where the processing chain overlaps—and intentionally different elsewhere.

Research on lightweight or virtual protective relays commonly combines IEC 61850 Sampled Values, digital signal processing, protection functions, and virtualized execution. ARVREL implements the SV-to-protection-to-evidence chain, but it does not currently claim transformer differential 87T, CPU-isolated deployment, certified real-time performance, or operational trip authority.

What ARVREL contributes now

  • Live or replayed IEC 61850 SV subscription
  • Source-visible fundamental phasor estimation
  • 50/51/50N/51N/67P/67N/27/59/59N
  • Transport, configuration, measurement, and trip trust separation
  • Reviewable virtual-operation evidence

What remains a research track

  • 87T multi-terminal differential protection
  • Vector-group and CT compensation
  • Inrush and CT-saturation restraint
  • CPU isolation and real-time scheduling benchmarks
  • HIL, IEC 60255, calibration, and conformance evidence

Research boundary

Automated software tests are necessary evidence, not certification.

Passing deterministic tests demonstrates repeatable behavior for the tested code and fixtures. It does not establish calibrated accuracy, hard-real-time determinism, IEC 60255 type testing, IEC 61850 conformance, commissioning acceptance, operational switching authority, or suitability as a production protection IED.
